don't get upsold

you went in for one thing. leave with one thing.

You go in to buy a $15 charger and somehow leave with a case, a screen protector and a two-year protection plan you'll never use.

Don't do this

  1. Jordan hey! what can I get you?
  2. Sam just a USB-C charger pls
  3. Jordan the fast one's only $10 more
  4. Sam oh ok sure
  5. Jordan want a 2-year protection plan on that?
  6. Sam …I guess?
  7. Jordan most people grab the cable bundle too. and if you join Rewards+ today you save 10%!
  8. Sam wait, how is it $67

Fast upgrade$10.00
2-year protection$12.99
Cable bundle$24.99
Rewards+, yearly$9.99
Member discount−$6.30
Total$66.67

Sam only wanted a charger. Each extra was a few dollars, so none of them felt like a big deal at the time, but by the end Sam was paying more than four times what they went in to spend.

Saying no to someone's face is a bit awkward, and saying yes is quicker. Shops know this, which is why they ask at the till when your card's already out.

Watch out for stuff like:

  • “It's only $3 more for the large.”
  • “Do you want to protect that?”
  • “Most people go for the bundle.”
  • “This deal ends today.”
  • “Sign up and save 10% on this order.”
  • “Start your free trial, cancel anytime.”
  • a “priority shipping” box that's ticked by default

Just buy what you went in for.

Do this instead

  1. Jordan hey! what can I get you?
  2. Sam just a USB-C charger pls
  3. Jordan the fast one's only $10 more
  4. Sam just the regular one, thanks!
  5. Jordan protection plan?
  6. Sam no thanks
  7. Jordan sure, that's $15
USB-C charger$15.00
Total$15.00

You don't have to explain yourself. Whoever's at the till asks everyone the same thing, and they really don't mind if you say no.

If just saying no feels rude, any of these work:

  • “Just this today, thanks!”
  • “Nah, I'm good with just this.”
  • “Not today, thanks.”
  • “How much is it without all the extras?”

And if you actually want the extra thing, fine, buy it. Just make sure it's because you wanted it and not because someone asked at the right moment.
