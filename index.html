Notice that Jordan never lied. Every single add-on was technically a fair offer, and each one only cost “a little more”. That's the whole trick: nobody would agree to a $96 charger, but almost anyone will agree to five small yeses in a row.

Upsells work because saying no feels rude, deciding fast feels efficient, and the thing you already agreed to makes the next thing feel small. None of that has anything to do with whether you actually need it.

Watch out for:

  • “It's only $3 more for the large.”
  • “Do you want to protect that?”
  • “Most people go for the bundle.”
  • “This deal ends today.”
  • “Want to sign up and save 10% on this order?”
  • “Start your free trial, cancel anytime.”
  • ☑ Add priority shipping (ticked for you)

Just buy the thing you came for! 😫

You don't owe anyone a reason. “No thanks” is a complete sentence, and the person offering has heard it a thousand times today. They're not offended, they're following a script.

If a flat no feels too blunt, you can soften it as much as you like:

  • “Just this today, thanks!”
  • “I'll stick with what I came for.”
  • “Not right now, I'll come back if I change my mind.”
  • “Can I see the price without any add-ons?”
  • “I'm good, appreciate it though.”

And if you genuinely want the extra? Great, get it! The point isn't to never buy anything. It's to decide on purpose, not because someone asked at the exact moment your card was already out.

👀 Spot the upsell

The decoy

Small $3, medium $6.50, large $7. The medium exists to make the large look like a steal.

“Only $X more”

Comparing against what you're already spending, not against zero. $10 more is still $10.

The protection plan

Priced on how scared you feel, not on how likely it is to break. Check what the warranty already covers.

The bundle

A discount on three things you'd never have bought separately is not a saving.

The pre-ticked box

Insurance, priority shipping, a newsletter. Read the checkout page before you hit pay.

The free trial

Free for 7 days, then quietly not. Set a reminder to cancel the moment you sign up.

The countdown

“Ends tonight!” If it was a good deal yesterday, it'll probably be one next week too.

The membership

Save 10% today by paying $39 a year. Do the maths before the cashier does it for you.

Buy what you came for. Keep the change. 🎉
